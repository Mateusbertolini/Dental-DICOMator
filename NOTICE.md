# Attribution notice

Dental DICOMator is a derivative adaptation of DICOMator, originally developed by Michael John James Douglass, for dental education and research.

Original publication:

Douglass MJJ. An open-source tool for converting 3D mesh volumes into synthetic DICOM CT images for medical physics research. Physical and Engineering Sciences in Medicine. 2025;48:1525-1538. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13246-025-01599-x

The dental adaptation was developed by Mateus Bertolini Fernandes and adds dental material behavior, painted layers, millimetre-based geometry, a dental interface, validation, and documentation.
